# PK45.03 — guild member record upsert

Date: 2026-11-04. Status: fixed variant framing, member fields and guild-window state mutation traced; record schema and unused-byte semantics remain open. No rows closed.

Opcodes 0x0176 and 0x0182 are each fixed at 106 bytes, routed through `HandleGuildWindowPacket` to `ro::guild::ReadWindowPacket`. The shared parser requires at least 106 bytes and treats the record as beginning at packet offset 2. It reads account u32@2, character u32@6, signed job u16@16, signed level u16@18, experience u32@20, online as `u32@24 != 0`, position u32@28, and bounded name[24]@82.

It upserts by the pair `(account, character)`, appending when absent or replacing the entire `Member` when present, then recomputes `state.online` by counting true member flags. Other `Member` fields such as gender/hair remain defaulted and bytes between the consumed fields and name are not parsed. The outer packet handler also recomputes local member position-derived permissions/title when available.

Open work: distinguish the server meaning of 0x0176 versus 0x0182 and validate offsets, numeric signedness and unused bytes against the selected source; determine whether replacement should preserve gender/hair or other prior values; replay add/update/duplicate members, online-count changes, partial/malformed payloads, and local position/permission effects. No independent fixture or production replay was performed for this leaf.

## Server-counterpart provenance escalation (2026-11-04)

Do not infer that 0x0154's member schema applies to 0x0176/0x0182. This direction/schema mismatch needs stronger protocol-history or alternate-revision review before any implementation or defect classification. No discovery row is closed.
