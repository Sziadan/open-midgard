## Coverage

The production `ConnectChar` integration now arms the prefix policy after connecting, captures the exact selected 17-byte `0x0065`, receives its raw `44332211` account-ID echo, then routes the following 24-byte empty `0x006B` response through `RecvPacket -> PollNetwork -> OnAcceptChar`. It reaches the production no-character status and CharSelect transition.

Direct production-connection replay adds 12 prefix-boundary records: AID `0x12345678` (`78 56 34 12`), `0xA1B2006C` (`6C 00 B2 A1`, fixed-opcode low-word collision), and `0xA1B20069` (`69 00 B2 A1`, variable-opcode low-word collision), each split after byte 1, 2, 3, and 4. Partial prefixes remain buffered and never produce framed packets. Full expected AIDs are consumed once, the following 24-byte character-list frame is preserved, and a second frame is received normally after the one-shot state clears.

Negative lifecycle cases reject a mismatched full AID before any following frame is delivered, close and clear the queue on EOF at zero through three prefix bytes, and reconnect the same connection object to ordinary `0x006C` framing without inheriting the old expectation. Fake `0x8482`/`0x8483` packet registrations are asserted absent. All 19 JSONL records are in `PK04.03-raw-aid-replay.jsonl`.
