The production replay captures the corresponding cancel-stage, stage, register, cancel-listing, bid, browse and own-list frames. It also captures the ten-byte `0288` cash purchase after payment confirmation. These eight discovery identities are C2S-only. Their receive results have separate identities.

Keep `025D` open as a bidirectional identity. Replay verifies a six-byte stop request and a four-byte close reply. The registered `025E` is only a length key in the pinned source. The exact source search `rg -ni '0x0?25e|ZC_AUCTION_ACK_MY_SELL_STOP' src` in rAthena finds the registry assignment and the close sender's length references. That sender writes `025D` at offset zero. Move `025E` to PK40 and classify its independent wire form as inactive for this source. Keep its server registry entry because the active close reply depends on the registered length.
