# PK45.04 — invitation-family source self-review

Date: 2026-11-04. Status: bounded client/source and selected-registry review complete; full package review, production replay, and row closure remain open.

## Reviewed slice

Compared the newly extended PK45.03 evidence for guild invitation (`0x016A`/`0x016B` and client result `0x0169`), guild alliance (`0x0171`/`0x0172`), friend invitation (`0x0207`/`0x0208`), and party invitation (`0x00FC`/`0x00FF`) against the client request/prompt paths and `PK01.02-server-registry-final.csv`.

The selected registry confirms these inbound reply identities and lengths:

- `0x016B`, 10 bytes, `clif_parse_GuildReplyInvite`.
- `0x0172`, 10 bytes, `clif_parse_GuildReplyAlliance`.
- `0x0208`, 14 bytes, `clif_parse_FriendsListReply`.
- `0x00FF`, 10 bytes, `clif_parse_ReplyPartyInvite`.
- `0x00FC`, 6 bytes, `clif_parse_PartyInvite`.

Client serialization matches those registered packet sizes and the traced offsets for prompt IDs, character IDs where applicable, decision flags, and target AID. Prompt consumption differs materially: commerce prompts clear on successful send but remain on send failure; the native party-accept window clears/closes before sending and has no retry state. Party-create options are sent separately after a success acknowledgement, and a failed options send is not surfaced by that path.

## Review limits and follow-up

This comparison checks client serialization and selected registry identity only. It does not inspect the selected server handler bodies for permission, state, decision polarity, or result semantics. No production replay or UI lifecycle run was performed in this bounded review, and no discovery row is closed. PK45.04 remains in progress pending the remaining registered-handler self-review and a usable production replay build/runtime.
