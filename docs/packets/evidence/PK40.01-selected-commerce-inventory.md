## Auction

`conf/battle/feature.conf` defaults `feature.auction` to off. The selected date compiles the legacy packet branches and permits enabling the feature. `clif_Auction_openwindow`, add-item, register, search, and own-list entry points check the runtime flag. Cancel, stop, and bid have different gates. Establish the deployed feature setting separately from protocol availability.

- `024B` is a four-byte create/cancel-selection request. The parser ignores the type, returns any staged item through the normal inventory path, and clears the staged quantity. `024C` sends an inventory index at 2 and a 32-bit count at 4. The server expects exactly one and subtracts two from the client index. `0256` is five bytes with a 16-bit index and a one-byte result. Success echoes the wire index. Some failures return the raw inventory index. Correlate replies to the pending item and leave inventory authoritative to its own updates.
- `024D` sends 32-bit starting and buy-now prices at 2 and 6 and 16-bit hours at 10. Validate price ordering, quantity and fee bounds before sending. `024E` and outbound `025D` each send a 32-bit auction ID in six bytes. `024F` sends auction ID and 32-bit bid in ten bytes. `0250` is a three-byte result with codes 0–9 for bid failure or success, cancellation and its rejection, registration limit, fee, win or loss, insufficient Zeny, and bid limit. Include results arriving outside the open window and preserve the regular inventory and Zeny owners.
- `0251` is 34 bytes with search type at 2, auction ID at 4, a 24-byte search string at 8, and page at 32. `025C` selects own sales or bids with a 16-bit value at 2. `0252` has a 12-byte header with 32-bit pages and row count. Each row is 83 bytes. Validate the declared length and exact row count before replacing results, and handle an empty result. The row contains auction ID and seller at 0 and 4, item ID/type/quantity at 28/30/34, flags and refine at 36–38, four 16-bit cards starting at 39, prices at 47 and 51, buyer at 55, and expiry epoch seconds at 79. Clear pending search and selection on close and map change.
- **Close reply collision.** `clif_Auction_close` reserves and commits the four-byte `025E` length, then writes `025D` as the opcode and a 16-bit result at 2. The selected executable routes decimal 605 to `Zc_Auction_My_Sell_Stop`, whose results are ended, rejected, and invalid ID. Frame inbound `025D` as four bytes and keep outbound `025D` at six. `025E` is a four-byte registration with no selected emitter found in this trace. Retain its discovery ID and route its applicability review through PK40.
- `025F` is a six-byte window command with a 32-bit type. The selected `clif_Auction_openwindow` emits zero for open after checking incompatible interfaces and the feature flag. The reference also consumes type one for close. Use `Ref/GameModePacket.cpp` and the auction window producers for list, staging, quote, and close ownership. Replay concatenated close replies and following packets, including a late result after local close.

## NPC cash shop

- `0287` has a 12-byte header with 32-bit cash and Kafra balances at 4 and 8. Each selected row is 11 bytes with 32-bit base and discount prices, a one-byte type, and 16-bit item ID. `ENABLE_OLD_CASHSHOP_PREVIEW_PATCH` is disabled in the selected configuration. Validate the full row span before changing shop state. The reference uses the existing shop and purchase windows with cash-specific state.
- `0288` is the effective ten-byte single-item buy request with 16-bit ID and amount and 32-bit Kafra contribution at 6. The earlier six-byte form and later variable multi-item form use separate profiles. `0289` is a 12-byte reply with both balances and a 16-bit result at 10. It refreshes both balances on success or rejection. Preserve ordinary inventory updates as the authority for acquired items. Verify discount prices, mixed balances, quantity and multiplication bounds, stale selections, duplicate replies, and teardown.
- `0846` reaches its tab parser only from MAIN or RE `20100824` or Zero. Its `08C0` reply branch further requires `20110222 <= PACKETVER < 20120000`. `0848` and the `08C9` request driving `08CA` enter later packet database blocks. The effective selected registry has none of these paths. `09AC` and `09AD` are cash bargain-sale lookup and result, with registration and `PACKETVER_SUPPORTS_SALES` gated at `20131223`. The `20130731` registry block nearby concerns a skill-entry packet.

## Banking

`09AB` checks the balance through `09A6`. `09A7` and `09A9` request deposit and withdrawal, with `09A8` and `09AA` returning the result, vault amount, and carried Zeny. `09B6`/`09B7` and `09B8`/`09B9` open and close the interface. The request registrations and reply bodies require `PACKETVER >= 20130717`. The selected registry excludes all ten identities. PK40.04 can adjudicate them as inactive for MAIN 20080910. Keep the cash-sale pair with PK40.03.
