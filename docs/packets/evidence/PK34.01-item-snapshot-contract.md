# PK34.01 item snapshot contract

The selected rAthena source sets `PACKETVER` and `PACKETVER_SAK_NUM` to `20080910` in `src/custom/defines_pre.hpp`. `packets_struct.hpp` selects inventory normal `02E8` and equipment `02D0`, cart normal `02E9` and equipment `02D2`, and storage normal `02EA` and equipment `02D1`. Each has a four-byte header with the inclusive length at offset 2. Storage has no name field at this version. The compiled [layout probe](PK34.01-item-snapshot-probe.cpp) checks the six opcodes and headers against the selected source. [Existing field probe](PK09.04-inventory-fields-probe.cpp) verifies the row offsets.

- Normal rows are 22 bytes. The signed 16-bit index and unsigned 16-bit item ID are at 0 and 2, type and identified at 4 and 5, signed 16-bit quantity at 6, allowed equip mask at 8, four 16-bit card words at 10, and a signed 32-bit expiration at 18.
- Equipment rows are 26 bytes. Index, ID, type and identified share the normal offsets. Allowed equip mask is at 6, actual wear mask at 8, damaged and refine at 10 and 11, four card words at 12, expiration at 20, and bind-on-equip at 24. `clif_item_equip` sends bind values 0 for unbound, 1 for bind on equip, and 2 for already bound. The quantity is implicit one.
- `clif.cpp::client_index` maps inventory and cart slots to indices 2–101. `client_storage_index` maps personal and guild storage slots to 1–600. These bounds are the selected header constants, independent of the advertised current occupied count and the per-character storage entitlement. The sender iterates each slot at most once. `item_data::isStackable` excludes weapon, armor, pet egg, pet armor, and shadow gear. `clif.cpp::itemtype` remaps pet eggs and shadow gear to armor or weapon display types, so those records still belong to the equipment category.

The older inventory variants are `00A3`, `01EE`, `00A4`, and `0295`. Older storage uses `00A5`, `0295`, `00A6`, and `0296` in the selected server's historical branches. The client also recognizes the Ref historical normal-storage alias `01F0`. Older cart variants are `0123`, `01EF`, `0122`, and `0297`. These compatibility paths have their own strides. `0295` is shared by two historical roles in current server source and has no selected emitter. Keep the existing dispatch scope until that alias is adjudicated separately.

## Lifecycle

`clif_inventorylist`, `clif_cartlist`, and `clif_storagelist` each omit empty categories. A selected frame covers one category. Inventory and storage have a generic signed-16-bit packet batching loop, but the configured capacities of 100 and 600 slots fit in one frame per category. Cart has 100 slots and sends one frame per nonempty category. The selected version has no explicit list start or end packet. Preserve the opposite category when an individual list arrives. A compatible header-only list explicitly clears its own category.

`clif_parse_LoadEndAck` and `clif_refresh` send inventory first, with `013C` ammo selection after the normal list when equipped. They send cart lists before cart info when the cart is active. Existing fresh-map and same-map reuse reset the previous items through `ResetForMapEntry`, which is necessary for omitted categories. The server also has unsolicited inventory refresh callers, including pet evolution. A missing category in that path has no independent reset signal. Keep that ambiguity explicit and check its delta ordering before attributing disappearance to a missing list.

`storage.cpp` opens personal, guild, and premium storage by sending lists before `00F2` count/capacity. `clif_refresh_storagewindow` repeats that order for an already-open storage. Individual storage additions and removals can also send `00F2`, so the count packet alone cannot clear or replace storage items. Existing close and map-entry paths clear storage before fresh lists can arrive. An empty refresh of an already-open storage has no category frame. Verify the `00F2` zero-count and refresh cases independently of ordinary count updates before changing storage lifecycle.

## Inventory implementation

The inventory receiver stages a complete category before publishing it, including historical layouts already routed to that receiver. A failed row leaves the previous lists, appearance, and pet egg selection intact. `pc_additem` can assign a new item to any vacant slot through `pc_search_inventory(sd,0)`. Since an omitted category does not acknowledge a vacancy, a valid incoming row supersedes a stale opposite-category row at that index. Other opposite-category rows survive. A normal list that replaces a worn equipment slot also rebuilds appearance. Valid equipment lists still remember the last eligible egg in wire order and a list without an eligible egg keeps the previous remembered index.

The production replay covers a partial row, bad or duplicate final indices, negative quantity, a zero item ID after a valid egg row, an upper-bound index, and cross-category slot reuse. Existing fixtures now use server-valid inventory indices. The packet and world-entry replays and 17 focused render checks passed.

## Cart and storage implementation

Inventory, cart, and storage share the list envelope and row validation. Cart stages a new map before merging the untouched category, with a fresh row taking precedence at a reused index. Storage stages its rows before replacing their category and clears worn flags. Storage list packets can arrive before `00F2` opens the window. A nonzero `00F2` updates counts without deleting rows. A zero `00F2` establishes that the entire storage is empty, including a refresh with both categories omitted. The existing cart and storage close and map-entry resets remain in use.

Production replay verifies malformed final cart and storage rows, header-only category clearing, storage lists before open, count updates, zero-count refresh, and close/reopen. The packet replay, world-entry handoff, and six item render checks passed. A nonempty refresh that omits one previously populated storage category still needs a source and ordering review in PK34.04.
