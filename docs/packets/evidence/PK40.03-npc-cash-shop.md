The receive path validates the entire catalog before replacing the NPC shop quote and opens the existing shop and purchase windows. Native and Qt purchase views show both balances. The discounted row price supplies the purchase total.

The shared numeric prompt accepts zero for cash-only payment. The client caps the requested contribution at the Kafra balance, rounds down to a multiple of 100, and caps it at the purchase total, in the reference order. The confirmation shows both amounts.
