# PK47.04 — Cross-stage legacy packets

## PK47.04-PT-ACCOUNT-LOGIN

The legacy PT account flow belongs to the login stage. `Ref/dhxj/packet.h:3553–3573` identifies the direction of every opcode in this slice, and `Ref/dhxj/LoginMode.cpp:1264–1290` places the receive flow behind `_PT_SERVER`. Its layouts are in `Ref/dhxj/packet.h:9437–9479`, with exact size registrations in `Ref/dhxj/packet.cpp:1037–1056`.

- `0261`–`0263` are S2C challenge frames with a nine-byte seed.
- `0264` and `0265` are C2S responses with two nine-byte fields. `0266` is a C2S response with a 28-byte card field.
- `0267`–`026D` are S2C failure responses with a 16-bit code.
- `026F` and `0270` are two-byte S2C messages for initial-login information. `0271` is the C2S account information reply and `0272` is the S2C PT identity information. The sibling `0271` struct and final selected map registry agree on 40 bytes. The map table's earlier 38-byte assignment at line 741 is overridden at line 747.

The selected map registry keeps the lengths with null parsers. Exact opcode and packet-symbol searches in the selected rAthena login, character and map source found no corresponding parser or sender. The discovery rows now identify the original login stage, with selected server inactivity as the disposition. Other profiles and their live login behavior remain open.

## PK47.04-LEGACY-BILLING

The two-byte `01BE` (`AC_ASK_PNGAMEROOM`) opens a confirmation there, and accepting writes the three-byte `01BF` (`CA_REPLY_PNGAMEROOM`) with answer 1 at offset 2. Declining disconnects. The selected rAthena map registry retains all three sizes with no parser, and the selected login and character sources have no corresponding parser or emitter.
