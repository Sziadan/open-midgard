# PK46.02 selected chat and social requests

Lengths and registered offsets come from the pinned MAIN 20080910 registry. Parser observations refer to the inspected local `clif.cpp` with SHA-256 `76c1c4202040daab90f2e441ffad05d9b9ce612d2a16edba55d2926c83ccbb6e`, which differs from the pin.

## PK46.02-CHAT-REQUESTS

- `00F3`, `0108`, and `017E` send global or room, party, and guild chat respectively. The four-byte header contains the two-byte total length at offset 2. The client appends the formatted name and message, terminated by NUL, at offset 4. The submission fixture captures each route, including room input and modifier overrides.
- `0096` sends a whisper with the same length header, a zero-padded 24-byte recipient at offset 4, and NUL-terminated text at offset 28. The submission and whisper fixtures exercise the recipient and payload framing.

## PK46.02-CHAT-ROOM-REQUESTS

- `00D5` and `00DE` are variable-length room creation and settings changes. Both carry the two-byte length at offset 2, capacity at 4, room type at 6, eight password bytes at 7, and title bytes at 15. The settings fixture captures both requests.
- `00D9` is a 14-byte join request with room GID at offset 2 and eight password bytes at 6. Replay captures public and private joins and checks retry pacing and pending entry ownership.
- `00E0` and `00E2` are 30-byte owner transfer and 26-byte member kick requests. Transfer sends a four-byte role at offset 2 and a 24-byte name at 6. The selected parser ignores the role field. Kick carries the 24-byte name at offset 2. The member-menu fixture captures both and checks that local roles and membership wait for server updates.
- `00E3` is a two-byte leave request. Replay captures the close action and checks that the room stays owned until server departure.

## PK46.02-FRIEND-REQUESTS

- `0202` is a 26-byte friend invitation with a 24-byte name field at offset 2. Replay captures the player and chat-room member actions.
- `0203` is a ten-byte removal request with account and character IDs at offsets 2 and 6. The messenger fixture captures confirmation, cancellation without a send, and server-confirmed row removal.
- `0208` is a 14-byte friend invitation reply with account ID, character ID and four-byte answer at offsets 2, 6 and 10. The inspected parser reads the account and answer and ignores the character field. Replay captures acceptance and decline and checks prompt ownership on send failure.

The crosswalk preserves discovery owners. Live membership, delivery and result handling, and independent PK48 review remain open.
