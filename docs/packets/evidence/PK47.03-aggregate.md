# PK47.03 — Source-backed historical identity aggregate

Date: 2026-11-04. Status: completed aggregate of bounded source scans; semantic identity and implementation-path questions remain open.

## Consolidated evidence

- The corrected snapshot-local catalog contains **1,380** unique opcodes. The PK47.02 crosswalk maps **938** rows to the 990-row discovery union; **442** have no discovery parent. Final selected-registry membership is **596 present / 784 absent**, with no membership discrepancies. These are inventory and presence facts, not protocol reachability.
- PK47.03.001 traced active `src/map/clif_packetdb.hpp` occurrences for **234** of the 442 catalog-only identities, totaling **299** guarded registration rows. Each row includes source location, preprocessor context, declared length/parser shape and selected registry observation. The occurrence split is **214** `parseable_packet` registrations with named parsers and **85** `packet` registrations without parsers. At opcode level, 152 have parser registrations, 81 have a null-parser registration, and one has both.
- The remaining **208** catalog-only identities have packet-definition declarations but no active map packetdb registration matched by the bounded scan. Declaration or registration absence is not proof of inactivity or lack of emit/receive paths.
- PK47.03.002 corrected the scanner to exclude comments/string literals and renamed the output field to `definitions_in_source_snapshot`. Four comment-derived false positives were removed: `0x0086`, `0x0926`, `0x092E`, and `0x095A`. The discovery/client row for `0x0086` is unaffected.

## Disposition and limits

All **234 registration identities** and **208 declaration identities** remain `UNRESOLVED`. The current work does not establish per-opcode sender reachability, parser semantics, exact layouts for raw-length registrations, original-client behavior, or deployment applicability. No discovery rows were added or closed, and no semantic classifications changed. PK47.04 must preserve these limits and can close only individually supported identities after further source-path evidence.
