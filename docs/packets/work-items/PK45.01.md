## Per-handler evidence record

Record opcode and discovery IDs; selected registration/guard/length/parser fields; final source emitter and call-site reachability; client receive registration and handler; exact state/UI/reply/effect/resource ownership transitions; reset/death/disconnect/map-change behavior; independent fixture provenance; production replay/UI scenario and outcome; unresolved profile/variant; row decision and evidence link.

## Entry gates and deferred boundaries

The package card seeds 240 registered entries and 136 indexed handler functions. Both lists need explicit contract references, and each applicable handler/variant needs an evidence-backed leaf.
